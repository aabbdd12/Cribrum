wikimedia-servers-amsterdam.jpg is a photograph by Dennis van Zijlekom
of the back of a Wikimedia server rack at the Amsterdam cluster
(file description: "The back of the new Wikimedia rack at the Amsterdam
cluster"), taken on 8 November 2006 and uploaded by Mark Bergsma.
