# Cribrum — Sources

The places the editor checks every week. The list is public so readers can judge where the paper looks, and where it does not.

## How sources are used

- **Primary sources** are cited: official announcements, documents, speeches, filings and papers.
- **Reliable press** is cited for reporting and for confirming primary sources.
- **Leads only** are never cited: newsletters, aggregators, social media posts and AI-written roundups. They point to stories; the story is then confirmed elsewhere.

## Primary sources

**AI developers** — official news pages of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta AI, Microsoft, Mistral AI, xAI / SpaceX AI, Nvidia, and the main Chinese labs (DeepSeek, Alibaba Qwen, Moonshot, Zhipu).

**Governments and regulators** — European Commission AI Office; UK government (GOV.UK) and the AI Security Institute; US White House, NIST and federal agencies; Government of Canada; state and city bodies when they act on AI (for example the New York City Council).

**Economic institutions** — IMF, World Bank, OECD, International Labour Organization, central banks.

**Research** — arXiv, Nature, Science, and the papers and repositories released by labs and universities.

## Reliable press

**Wire services and general press** — Reuters, Associated Press, AFP, Bloomberg, Financial Times, The New York Times, The Washington Post, The Guardian, Le Monde, El País.

**Technology press** — Ars Technica, The Verge, TechCrunch, Wired, The Register, MIT Technology Review.

**Specialist technical press** — Phoronix, PetaPixel, Help Net Security, BleepingComputer. These often break stories about software and security before the general press does.

**Science press** — Nature News, Science News.

## Leads only

AI newsletters and weekly digests, news aggregators, Hacker News, Reddit, posts on X, Bluesky and LinkedIn.

## Lessons from the first issues

- Weekly roundups recirculate old stories: Meta's 16,000 job cuts, reported in March, appeared in October roundups as new. The event date is always checked at the source.
- An acquisition announced on September 28 appeared in an October 12 digest. Same rule.
- The ArtCraft story broke in the technical press first. That press is now on the list.
